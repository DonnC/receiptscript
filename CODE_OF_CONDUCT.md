# Code Of Conduct

ReceiptScript aims to be a welcoming project for people building practical software in constrained environments.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, especially when reviewing early ideas.
- Give feedback that helps the work improve.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Publishing private information without consent
- Repeated disruptive behavior after being asked to stop

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior harms the community.
